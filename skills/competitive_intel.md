# Competitive Battle Cards: Explorers Consulting (AI agent setup service) and Explorers App (AI-native small business platform, launching March 2027)

Use these when a prospect mentions a competitor or asks "how are you different?"

## Market Positioning
Explorers positions itself as AI that does the work, on a business organized for it. Instead of another app or chat window, it builds one organized, permissioned Brain for a small business and runs its AI, apps and agents on top. Two entry points: Explorers Consulting, a service available now that sets up agents and the file systems and processes behind them for service and trade businesses of 5 to 50 people in New England, and Explorers App, the platform launching March 2027. Consulting finds the business, scopes the work and sets the team up, then moves them onto the App. The tone stresses owner control, with nothing leaving the building without the owner's OK.

## Our Unique Strengths
- A service-to-platform path: Consulting sets up agents today and migrates the customer onto Explorers App at launch
- One organized, permissioned Brain that the team, its AI and its agents all work from, instead of another application
- Echo answers only from the owner's own files and cites its sources, and JEV shows where every file went and why
- Human-in-the-loop by design: agents stop for the owner's OK, start read-only, and show how each run got its result
- Fixes the root cause of agent drift by organizing files and process first, with a retainer to keep agents tuned
- Pricing is setup per seat plus a monthly AI bill positioned below the cost of running your own AI, with upgrades as AI improves
- Focused on small service and trade businesses, with written setup guides and read-only integrations with NetSuite and QuickBooks

## Common Alternatives (Non-Competitors)
What prospects do instead of buying a solution like ours:
- Using ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini as a chat window with no business context
- Leaving the business scattered across email, spreadsheets, a CRM and the owner's head
- Building Zapier or Make automations in-house, or having a staff member or freelancer maintain them
- Hiring a custom AI agency or developer for a one-off build
- Doing nothing and handling quote follow-up, briefings, lookups and paper forms manually
- Adopting an all-in-one suite like HubSpot or Zoho and entering data by hand

## vs. ChatGPT / Microsoft Copilot / Google Gemini

**How we win:** These tools are chat windows that know nothing about the business. Explorers organizes the company's files into one permissioned Brain, so Echo answers only from the owner's own files, with sources. Explorers Consulting sets up agents that do real recurring work (quote follow-up, email briefings, parts lookup), and nothing goes out without the owner's OK.

**Their weakness:** They start from zero on every business, stay a chat window, and depend on the user's files and tools being organized. Agents built on top by hand drift as files change, and nobody maintains what they read.

**Migration angle:** Keep the AI tools you already pay for. We organize your business once so they stop starting from scratch, then build agents that do the work instead of just answering questions.

## vs. HubSpot

**How we win:** Explorers App is built around one organized Brain for the whole business, not a CRM with other tools around it. It pulls in email, spreadsheets, documents and exports, and agents and apps run on that. Pricing is a one-time setup per seat plus a monthly bill for the AI work, and setup is done for the customer through Consulting.

**Their weakness:** Suites like this hold only the data entered into them, so the business stays scattered across email, spreadsheets and the owner's head. They tend to be heavy for 5 to 50 person service and trade businesses, and AI is an added feature rather than the foundation.

**Migration angle:** Explorers plans to replace the stack later in 2027, with CRM-type apps and more agents. Start with Consulting on the tools you have today, then move onto one platform where the Brain, agents and reports all run together.

## vs. Zapier / Make

**How we win:** Explorers works on the underlying problem of unorganized files and process, then builds agents that read from a clean base. A retainer keeps agents tuned as files, tools and the business change, and agents start read-only and earn access as they prove themselves.

**Their weakness:** Automation platforms connect apps but need someone to design and maintain the workflows. Rule-based flows are brittle when inputs and files change, and they do not organize the business data itself.

**Migration angle:** Keep the integrations that work, and add agents that handle judgment-based work like drafting follow-ups and answering from files, with written setup guides so the team is not dependent on one builder.
