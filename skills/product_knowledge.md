# Product Knowledge: Explorers Consulting (AI agent setup service) and Explorers App (AI-native small business platform, launching March 2027)

## What We Sell
Explorers builds an AI-native platform and runs a consulting service that help small businesses get AI agents doing real work. Consulting organizes a business's files and processes, then builds and maintains agents on top of them. Explorers App is a platform where one organized, permissioned Brain powers the team's AI, apps, reports and agents. It is built for service and trade businesses with 5 to 50 people.

## Core Value Proposition
Explorers organizes a small business once so AI agents can reliably do the work on top of it, with the owner approving everything that goes out.

## Key Benefits
- Agents that reliably do real work because they run on a clean, organized file system and repeatable process
- Nothing goes out without the owner's OK; agents start read-only and earn more access over time
- Ongoing retainer keeps agents tuned as files, tools and the business change, preventing drift
- Team onboarding and written setup guides so agents become part of the daily workflow
- Clear path from consulting onto Explorers App, replacing a scattered stack with one organized Brain

## Features
- Brain: one organized, permissioned library where every file is sorted with a record of where it went and why
- JEV: organizes, sorts, summarizes and files uploaded documents, spreadsheets and exports, and explains why
- Echo: assistant that answers only from the business's own files, cites sources, and drafts new apps and agents
- Apps and Reports: live views of the business such as quotes, money owed and maintenance
- Agents: named workers that handle recurring work and stop for owner approval
- Explorers API: usage-based AI layer billed monthly

## Use Cases
- Quote follow-up: find quotes that went quiet and draft follow-ups for reps to send
- Morning email briefing summarizing overnight messages and what needs an answer
- Parts and vendor lookup by part number returning specs, price and supplier
- Personal assistant for the owner via phone with lists and reminders
- Paper-to-digital inspection and job forms filled on tablets and filed automatically
- Live reports on quotes, money owed and maintenance

## Target Audience
Owners and operators of service and trade businesses with 5 to 50 people, starting in New England, who want AI to do real work but have scattered files and tools (email, CRM, spreadsheets, SEO tools). Explorers App investors and early-adopter businesses are a secondary audience.

## Ideal Buyer Persona
A hands-on owner or senior operator of a 5-50 person service or trade business in New England who wants AI to actually do work, a JARVIS-like assistant, but whose business lives across email, a CRM, spreadsheets, and their own head. They run on Microsoft 365, Outlook, Google Workspace, and often NetSuite or QuickBooks. They have tried AI tools that were just chat windows with no knowledge of the business, and they lack the time or technical staff to organize files and maintain agents. They want quotes followed up, a morning email briefing, fast parts and vendor lookups, paper job forms digitized, and a personal assistant on their phone. Control matters to them: nothing goes out without their OK, and agents start read-only. Success is fewer dropped quotes and repetitive tasks, a team that trusts and uses the agents daily, and a clean path to one organized system instead of a scattered stack.

## Prospect Pain Points
- Business information is scattered across email, CRM, spreadsheets, SEO tools, and the owner's head
- AI tools tried so far know nothing about the business, so AI stays a chat window
- Quotes go quiet and follow-up slips, costing revenue
- Agents set up by hand drift as files change and nobody maintains them
- Paper inspection and job forms are slow and hard to file
- Looking up parts, specs, pricing, and suppliers takes too long
- Owner is overloaded with inbox and task management and lacks time or in-house technical staff for AI

## Buying Triggers
When these events happen, prospects are most likely to buy:
- Quotes or leads going unanswered or being lost to slow follow-up
- Growth or hiring that makes owner-dependent processes and scattered files unmanageable
- A failed or disappointing attempt at using ChatGPT or other AI tools, or pressure to adopt AI before competitors
- Moving off paper forms, or a CRM or ERP change such as a NetSuite or QuickBooks rollout

## Disqualifiers
Do NOT pursue prospects who:
- Fewer than 5 employees or more than roughly 50, or an enterprise with a dedicated IT and AI team
- Located outside New England, since the consulting service is New England first
- Not a service or trade business, such as pure e-commerce or a software company
- Owner wants fully unsupervised AI with no approval steps, or has no files, tools, or recurring processes to organize

## Market Positioning
Explorers positions itself as AI that does the work, on a business organized for it. Instead of another app or chat window, it builds one organized, permissioned Brain for a small business and runs its AI, apps and agents on top. Two entry points: Explorers Consulting, a service available now that sets up agents and the file systems and processes behind them for service and trade businesses of 5 to 50 people in New England, and Explorers App, the platform launching March 2027. Consulting finds the business, scopes the work and sets the team up, then moves them onto the App. The tone stresses owner control, with nothing leaving the building without the owner's OK.
